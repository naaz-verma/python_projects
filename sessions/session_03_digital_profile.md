# Session 3: Build Your Digital Profile

**Type:** Guided workshop (mixed students -- all course tracks)
**Duration:** 2 hours
**Audience:** Mixed -- Python, Digital Marketing, Graphics/Design students
**Format:** PPT + discussion + hands-on (LinkedIn & Resume)
**Goal:** Every student walks out with an optimized LinkedIn profile and a ready resume
**PPT:** `presentations/WorldWithWeb_Session_03_DigitalProfile.pptx`
**Prerequisite:** None -- open to all

---

## Session Flow (2 Hours)

### Part 1: Why Your Digital Profile Matters (15 min)

#### 1.1 Opening Hook: "The Invisible Problem" (5 min)

> "Two students. Same skills. Same college. Same CGPA.
> Student A has nothing online.
> Student B has LinkedIn, GitHub, and a portfolio.
> Who gets the call?"

**Show of hands:** "How many have a LinkedIn? Updated in last 3 months? Any portfolio online?"

#### 1.2 What Is a Digital Profile? (5 min)

| Platform | Best For | Who Checks It |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Professional identity | Recruiters, HR, clients |
| **GitHub** | Code & projects | Tech recruiters, developers |
| **Portfolio Website** | Your brand, your way | Everyone who Googles you |
| **Behance/Dribbble** | Design & visual work | Design agencies, clients |
| **Notion Portfolio** | Case studies, projects | Freelance clients, interviewers |

*"You don't need all of these. But you need at least one. Today we start with the two most important: LinkedIn and your Resume."*

#### 1.3 Real Examples (5 min)

Show on screen (prepare beforehand):
1. A strong student LinkedIn profile (good headline, banner, about, featured)
2. A GitHub profile with green graph and profile README
3. A Notion or website portfolio
4. A Behance portfolio with design projects

For each: what makes it good, how it's different from having nothing.

---

### Part 2: Platform Awareness -- Beyond LinkedIn (20 min)

Cover these as awareness. Not building today, but students should know what exists and what's relevant for their track.

#### 2.1 GitHub -- Your Code Portfolio (5 min)

**For:** Python students, anyone who codes

- GitHub = your code portfolio, like Instagram but for builders
- Profile README = your landing page on GitHub
- Green contribution graph = proof you're active and consistent
- Good READMEs on repos = professionalism
- 100M+ developers use it, recruiters check it before interviews

**Show:** A real GitHub profile with profile README and 5+ repos.

*"If you code, GitHub is non-negotiable. We have a separate hands-on session for this."*

#### 2.2 Portfolio Websites (5 min)

**For:** Everyone, especially DM and Graphics students

Options available (free or cheap):
| Platform | Effort | Best For |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Notion** | Low -- no coding, free | Everyone, quick portfolio |
| **Carrd.co** | Low -- drag & drop, free tier | Simple one-page site |
| **GitHub Pages** | Medium -- needs HTML/CSS | Python/web students |
| **WordPress** | Medium-High -- full site | DM students (SEO practice too) |
| **Bio.link / Linktree** | Very low | Quick link-in-bio page |

*"A portfolio website is your next step after today. Pick one that matches your comfort level."*

#### 2.3 Behance & Dribbble -- Visual Showcase (3 min)

**For:** Graphics and Design students

- Behance = Adobe's platform, industry standard for designers
- Dribbble = more curated, invite-based community
- Upload poster designs, logos, social media graphics, brand work
- Clients and agencies actively browse these to find designers

*"If you're in graphics, Behance is your GitHub. Start uploading your best work."*

#### 2.4 Other Things You Can Do (5 min)

| What | Why It Helps |
|------|-------------|
| **Write LinkedIn posts** | Shows you're active, builds visibility, algorithm rewards consistency |
| **Get recommendations on LinkedIn** | Social proof from trainers, peers, clients |
| **Contribute to open source** | Shows initiative (for coders) |
| **Start a blog** | Hashnode, Medium, or your own site -- shows thought leadership |
| **Build in public** | Share your learning journey -- people connect with progress |
| **Collect testimonials** | From project collaborators, clients, mentors |
| **Google yourself** | Check what comes up -- then fix it |

*"Your digital profile isn't a one-time thing. It's something you keep building. Today we lay the foundation."*

#### 2.5 Quick Mention: Resume & Jobs (2 min)

*"Everything we're building today -- LinkedIn, resume, the platforms we discussed -- becomes incredibly powerful when you start applying for jobs, internships, or freelance work. A strong digital profile is the first filter. We'll do a dedicated career and job-readiness session later. Today is about making yourself visible."*

---

### Part 3: Hands-On -- LinkedIn + Resume (60 min)

**This is the core. Every student builds. Same activity for all tracks.**

---

#### 3.1 LinkedIn Profile Optimization (30 min)

Everyone does this together, step by step on their screens.

**Step 1: Account & Photo (5 min)**
- Create LinkedIn account if needed
- Upload a clear profile photo (take one now against a plain wall if needed)
- No selfies, no group photos, no filters

**Step 2: Banner Image (5 min)**
- Go to Canva > search "LinkedIn banner"
- Pick a clean template
- Add your name + what you do (e.g., "Python Developer | AI Enthusiast")
- Download and upload to LinkedIn

**Step 3: Headline (5 min)**

Don't just write "Student at XYZ College." Use this format:

| Track | Headline Template |
|-------|------------------|
| Python | `Python Developer | AI & Automation | Building Real Projects` |
| DM | `Digital Marketing | SEO & Social Media | Content Strategy` |
| Graphics | `Graphic Designer | Brand & Visual Design | Canva & Adobe` |
| General | `Tech & Digital Skills | Building at WorldWithWeb | Open to Learn` |

**Step 4: About Section (10 min)**

Template for everyone:

```
Hi! I'm [Name], a [course/field] student at [College].

I'm currently learning [2-3 skills] at WorldWithWeb, where I'm building hands-on projects in [area].

I'm passionate about [1-2 interests] and actively looking to connect with professionals and fellow learners in this space.

Skills: [list 4-5 key skills]

Let's connect!
```

**Step 5: Add Details (5 min)**
- Add WorldWithWeb under Education
- Add 3-5 skills in the Skills section
- Set profile to "Open to opportunities" (optional)

---

#### 3.2 Resume Building (30 min)

**Tool:** Canva (free, works for all tracks, produces a polished PDF)

**Step 1: Open Canva Resume Template (3 min)**
- Go to canva.com (create account if needed)
- Search "resume" > pick a clean, professional template
- Avoid overly flashy designs -- clean and readable wins

**Step 2: Fill in Sections (25 min)**

Guide students through each section:

**Header:**
- Full name (large)
- One-line tagline (same as LinkedIn headline)
- Email | Phone | LinkedIn URL | City

**Education:**
- College name, degree, year
- WorldWithWeb -- [Course name] (current)

**Skills:**

| Track | Skills to List |
|-------|---------------|
| Python | Python, AI/ML Basics, API Integration, Streamlit, Git & GitHub, Problem Solving |
| DM | SEO, Social Media Marketing, Google Analytics, Content Writing, Canva, AI Tools |
| Graphics | Canva, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Brand Design, Social Media Graphics, Video Editing |

**Projects (most important section):**

For each project, use this format:
```
Project Name
- One line: what it is
- One line: what you did / your role
- One line: tools/tech used
```

If students don't have projects yet:
- Use coursework or assignments
- Mention "in progress" projects
- Write about skills being learned

**Extracurriculars / Certifications:**
- WorldWithWeb program
- Any online course certificates
- College activities, volunteering

**Step 3: Download (2 min)**
- Download as PDF
- Name the file: `YourName_Resume.pdf`
- Share in class WhatsApp group

---

#### Trainer Moves During Hands-On

- Walk the room continuously
- Help with account creation issues
- Review headlines and about sections -- give live feedback
- Help students pick good Canva templates
- Take screenshots of best profiles to showcase
- Keep energy up: "Who's done with their headline? Show me!"

---

### Part 4: Showcase & Wrap-Up (15 min)

#### 4.1 Profile Showcase (5 min)

Pick 2-3 students to share screen:
- Best LinkedIn transformation (before vs after)
- Best resume design

Quick class feedback. Celebrate the progress.

*"An hour ago, none of this existed. Now it's live and ready."*

#### 4.2 The Connection Challenge (5 min)

Right now, in the room:
1. Connect with 5 people sitting here on LinkedIn
2. Connect with the trainer
3. Send ONE connection request to someone you admire in your field (with a personal note)

#### 4.3 What's Next (5 min)

| This Week | This Month | Later |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Post on LinkedIn about today's session | Set up GitHub (coders) or Notion portfolio (others) | Build a personal website |
| Connect with 10 more people | Write one LinkedIn post about your learning | Google your name -- own page 1 |
| Send resume to one person for feedback | Get a LinkedIn recommendation | Explore Behance/Dribbble (designers) |

**Closing line:** *"Your skills are real. Now the world can see them."*

## Common Issues & Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No professional photo | Take a quick photo against a plain wall right now |
| LinkedIn phone verification | Use their number, help with OTP |
| Canva blocked on college Wi-Fi | Use mobile hotspot |
| "I have no projects" | Write about skills being learned, coursework, goals |
| "I don't know what to write" | Use the templates -- fill in blanks, don't write from scratch |
