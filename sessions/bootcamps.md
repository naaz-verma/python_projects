# Weekend Skill Sprint Bootcamps

**Format:** 8 hours (1 full day or 2x4 hrs) | **Audience:** College students & professionals | **Type:** Paid, repeatable

---

## Common Structure (All Bootcamps)

| Block | Duration | Content |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 1 | 1 hr | Industry context + career landscape |
| 2 | 5 hrs | Hands-on build + live project |
| 3 | 1 hr | Portfolio piece / certification / proof of work |
| 4 | 1 hr | Industry skills + roadmap + next steps |

---

## Bootcamp 1: Automation Flow Weekend

**Tags:** Automation, AI, No-Code, Freelancing

**Modules:**
1. Automation in real companies (ops, HR, marketing, finance)
2. Tools: n8n / Zapier / Google Workspace
3. Build automation flows:
   - Lead capture --> Email --> Sheet
   - Invoice / report automation
4. AI inside automation (ChatGPT + prompts)
5. Deployment & client delivery basics
6. Portfolio case study
7. Industry roles & freelancing paths

**Output:** 2 working automations + portfolio-ready case study
